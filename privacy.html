Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy describes how SQ Consulting handles information when you use The Daily Pulse at thedailypulse.space. The Daily Pulse is a free one-page news digest that gathers public headlines.

Information we handle

The hosting provider may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address, browser type, and the time of a request, to operate and secure the site. If you submit a subscription or advertising enquiry form, we store the details you enter so we can respond.

Advertising

Public pages may show third-party display advertisements from Adsterra. Those ads load only after you accept advertising cookies in the consent banner. The ad network may set its own cookies and collect technical data about the request in order to serve and measure ads. We do not control the individual creatives.

Analytics

We use cookieless analytics. The first-party pageview beacon records the path, referrer, viewport size, and time. It does not set a cookie or a lasting device identifier. If a GoatCounter site code is configured, the same kind of cookieless pageview is also sent to GoatCounter, a privacy-oriented analytics service that does not use cookies.

Cookies and similar storage

The consent banner stores your advertising-cookie choice in your browser so the site can remember it. Advertising cookies are used only after you accept. Details are on the cookie policy.
